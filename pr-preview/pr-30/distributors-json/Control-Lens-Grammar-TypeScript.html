Description

TypeScript's declaration files, .d.ts, like the TypeScript compiler, in two layers: tsTokensGrammar of their tokens, by their TsTokenCategorys, & tsGrammar of their declarations, of those tokens, by the compiler's names of their nodes, so that they parse & print together.

Both layers parse by parsecG & print by unparsecG, locating failures, since tsTokensGrammar scans tokens without their trivia, as the compiler does. Here, a declaration prints as tokens, & as characters, & parses back.

>>> :set -XOverloadedStrings
>>> let point = ObjectType [PropertySignature [] (PropertyIdentifier "x") False (Just (KeywordType NumberKeyword))]
>>> let printed = unparsecG tsGrammar [TypeAliasDeclaration [ExportKeyword] "Point" [] point] ([] :: [TsToken])
>>> let source = parsecStream (unparsecG tsTokensGrammar (parsecStream printed) ("" :: String))
>>> putStr source
export type Point = { x: number };
>>> parsecResult (parsecG tsGrammar (concat (parsecResult (parsecG tsTokensGrammar source)))) == Just [TypeAliasDeclaration [ExportKeyword] "Point" [] point]
True

tsGrammar :: CtxGrammar TsToken [TsDeclaration] Source #

The declarations of a declaration file, of tokens, by the TypeScript compiler's syntax, printed one per line, indented within blocks, & with object types on one line. Trivia print conventionally, & parse only from tokens with trivia, by parseG, since tsTokensGrammar scans without them. Its ;s print, & parse if they're there.

Printing a file, of many tokens, is linear into a stream which grows at its end in constant time, like a Seq, rather than a list.

tsTokenGrammar :: RegGrammar Char TsToken Source #

A token, scanned from its characters. Its trivia, whitespace, newlines & comments, are tokens, as the scanner of the TypeScript compiler scans without skipping them. A } is never a template's middle or tail, which tsTokensGrammar rescans in its context, as the compiler's parser rescans it.

A number is spelled by its digits, letters, separators & points, as in 0xFF, 1_000.5 or 1n, but not by a signed exponent.

tsTokensGrammar :: CtxGrammar Char [TsToken] Source #

Tokens, scanned from their characters without their trivia, as the TypeScript compiler's scanner skips trivia, & printed with them, as tsGrammar prints them, so that tsGrammar parses by LL(1). A } which closes a template's substitution is scanned as the template's middle or tail, by the depth of braces within the substitutions open, as the compiler's parser rescans it.
