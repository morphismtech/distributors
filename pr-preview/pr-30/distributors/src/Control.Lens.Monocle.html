{- | `Monocle`s are an optic that generalizes
`Control.Lens.Traversal.Traversal`s & `Control.Lens.Grate.Grate`s.

`Monocle`s are isomorphic to `Monocular`s.
-}

  (Monoidal p, Applicative f)
