# distributors-grammar
## Unifying Parsers, Printers & Grammars

[Github](https://github.com/morphismtech/distributors)

Grammars by the profunctor algebra of
[distributors](https://hackage.haskell.org/package/distributors):
one grammar parses, prints, generates & describes its language,
as a regular expression or a Backus-Naur form.
Start at "Control.Lens.Grammar".
